Hypothesis access

Why you might see “Group unavailable”

Our model predictions are stored in a private Hypothesis group. If your Hypothesis account is not a member of the group, Hypothesis shows “Group unavailable”.

Step 1 — Log in to Hypothesis

Make sure you’re logged in at hypothes.is using the Hypothesis account you want to use.

Open Hypothesis login Create Hypothesis account

Step 2 — Open the Model Predictions group

If you can’t view it, you need to be added to the group.

Open Model Group ({{ model_gid }})

Request access

Click this to send the admin a request to add your Hypothesis account to the model group. (Tip: include your Hypothesis username or email in the message.)

Step 3 — Set your personal workspace group

Your personal coding should go into your own private workspace group so it doesn’t affect gold or model annotations.

Set workspace group
