Reviewer access

Reviewers join the Hypothesis review group, then request review access here with their Hypothesis username. Human annotations from pending, unapproved, or blocked Hypothesis accounts are ignored during sync.

Reviewers can find their Hypothesis username in their Hypothesis account/profile. They only need to give you the username, for example alice; the app stores it as acct:alice@hypothes.is.
